Lydon Murtha, a former Minnesota high school football star who went on to play in the National Football League, died on Nov. 21 at the age of 41.

His obituary recalls a family man and “an avid fisherman.” The Lakeville resident had moved home from Wellington, Florida, in 2022 and leaves his wife and three children. No cause of death has been disclosed.

At Hutchinson High School, the 6-foot-7 tackle was a 2003 first-team all-state pick, a Class 4A runner-up and Minnesota’s Gatorade Player of the Year. He and Nate Swift became Nebraska’s first Minnesota prep signees since 1995, and he also suited up in the U.S. Army All-American Game.

He played 40 games for Nebraska from 2005 to 2008, starting eight in 2007 and nine in 2008. The Lions drafted him in the seventh round in 2009 and later traded him to the Dolphins. His career ended in 2011.
