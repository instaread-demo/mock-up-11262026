Toronto Blue Jays Top 10 Prospects For 2027 Fantasy Baseball

1. SS Jojo Parker | 20 | A+ | 2029

At 6’2” 200 pounds from the left side, Parker generates plus bat speed and has power to all fields and draws comps to Corey Seager: an outcome that would of course thrill the Blue Jays. The eighth overall pick in 2025, Parker slashed .270/.399/.460 with 13 home runs and 28 stolen bases in 108 games across two levels. Those numbers are impressive enough, but they look even better in the context of Parker improving throughout the year and batting .316 in 21 High-A games to close out the season.

 

2. LHP Johnny King | 20 | AA | 2027

A 2024 third-rounder, the 6’3” 210-pound lefty has added muscle and velocity, and his fastball-curveball pairing stifles lefties. A 2.95 ERA and 27 strikeouts in 21 Double-A New Hampshire innings point to 2027, barring an injury that could push him to 2028.

 

3. RHP Nolan Perry | 23 | AA | 2027

Perry, a 2022 12th-rounder from a New Mexico high school, overcame Tommy John surgery and a 104-mph liner to the head to become a consensus top-100 name. His 2026: 2.50 ERA, 1.01 WHIP and 117 strikeouts in 82.2 innings over three levels.

 

4. 3B Sean Keys | 23 | MLB | 2026

New bat speed powered a .294/.417/.604 line with 28 homers, seven steals and a 22.3 percent strikeout rate over 100 Double-A and Triple-A games. His 25-game Toronto debut was too brief to judge.

 

5. 2B SS OF Jay Harry | 24 | AAA | 2027

After a joke at his surname’s expense, The Itch notes Harry played shortstop, second and all three outfield spots in 2026, hitting .298/.343/.495 with 14 homers and one steal in 105 games. A big-league utility role is within reach.

 

6. LHP Cole Carlon | 21 | NCAA | 2028

The 6’5” 230-pound lefty moved from Arizona State’s bullpen to its rotation as a junior, logging 133 strikeouts, 31 walks and a 3.87 ERA in 83.2 innings before going 39th overall. A high-90s fastball sets up a diving slider.

 

7. 2B Elaineiker Coronado | 19 | DSL | 2031

The 5’10” lefty hit .315/.516/.472 with two homers and five steals in 32 Dominican Summer League games, with a 27.9%-to-10.1% rate split. He looks ready for Low-A, and a Double-A debut late in 2027 is possible.

 

8. C Ryan Sprock | 22 | A+ | 2028

A Twins eighth-rounder and former Elon two-way player, Sprock converted to catcher and hit .300/.422/.469 with 11 homers and 16 steals in 98 games, walking 16.3% and striking out 13.3%. That zone control suits Toronto.

 

9. C Will Brick | 18 | CPX | 2031

A fourth-round prep catcher, Brick signed for $2.5 million, second-round money. The 6’2” 195-pounder reclassified to enter the draft a year early after strong USA Baseball showings; The Itch passes on a name joke.

 

10. C Juan Caricote | 18 | DSL | 2032

Caricote, a strong defender, got $1.9 million, Toronto’s top 2026 international bonus. His DSL debut: 99 wRC+, a 16.4 percent strikeout rate, five homers and a .274/.369/.433 line. The glove should carry him while the swing develops.
