The smartest marketers know when to step away from the dashboard

AI and martech offer new ways to get closer to customers, but nothing beats getting out there and meeting them.

By Rob Angell|25 Nov 2026
Share this article

I will never forget the words of a sales director when I tried to brief him on a client satisfaction survey. I was a lowly market research executive at a GIS company and his response chilled me:

“You really need to get out there more if you think that’s right.”

It is a lesson Angell argues still holds. Marketers today have more ways than ever to watch customers from a distance, with AI tools and martech platforms promising a detailed view of behaviour and sentiment from behind a screen.

Those tools can be genuinely useful, he suggests, but a dashboard only shows what someone decided to measure. It rarely captures the context behind a decision, the words customers actually use or the frustrations they never think to mention in a survey.

His case is that the smartest marketers treat the data as a starting point rather than the whole picture, and make time to step away from it: meeting customers in person and seeing first-hand how they use what they buy, so that what they learn sharpens how they read everything else.
