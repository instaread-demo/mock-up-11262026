Project Play: MVC Coaching Summit Recap

Listen to audio version of this article
0:00 / 0:00 Instaread
Sponsored by

In partnership with Center for Healing and Justice through Sport (CHJS) and The DICK’S Sporting Goods Foundation, GameChanger hosted the Most Valuable Coach (MVC) Coach Summit in Boston, Mass. as part of a the MVC campaign that was launched earlier this year at the Project Play Summit.The Coach Summit brought together more than 50 coaches from 35 organizations across 11 states for a day of healing-centered training and hands-on learning.

Attendees spanned 19 sports, among them soccer, squash, flag football, rowing, golf and adaptive sports.

Their new skills should reach more than 8,600 young athletes; a short video from the day follows.
