Manly's halves conundrum has taken another twist after rising five-eighth Onitoni Large ruptured his ACL in preseason training.

The Sea Eagles confirmed on Wednesday that Large is expected to miss a significant portion of the 2027 NRL season after suffering the injury in training.

The situation looms as not only a blow to Manly's halves stocks, but adds a further complication around the potential plans to release Jamal Fogarty to Wests Tigers.

Coach Kieran Foran said he was "shattered for him" but hopes Large returns late in the year.

Seen as the club's future with halfback Joey Walsh, the 19-year-old is likely out for half of next season, as ACL ruptures need six months or more to heal.

Manly had hoped to free cap space for Walsh by moving on Fogarty or Luke Brooks. Large re-signed for two years last month with brother Ashton.

Under cap pressure, captain Tom Trbojevic (signed to the end of 2027) rejected a two-year extension, and lock Caleb Navale will leave for the PNG Chiefs after next year.
