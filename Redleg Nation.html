Reds lock up Tyler Stephenson with a new 3-year contract

Doug Gray 11/24/2026 92
Listen to audio version of this article
0:00 / 0:00 Instaread
Sponsored by

The Cincinnati Reds have made their first signing of the offseason and it’s a rather important one. Catcher Tyler Stephenson is returning to the team, signing a 3-year deal worth $30,000,000 according to Jeff Passan of ESPN. The contract is set up to allow Stephenson to opt out after two years.

Gray argues the impact outweighs the modest price: without Stephenson, the 40-man roster had only Jose Trevino and Will Banfield at catcher, and he sees neither as an everyday starter.

He counts only about 12 true starting catchers for 30 clubs. With Ryan Jeffers taking a qualifying offer, Stephenson, Cincinnati’s 1st round pick in 2015, was the lone starter-level free agent, and Gray suspects the Reds outbid rivals.

Now 30, Stephenson has spent seven seasons with the Reds, playing eight games in 2020 before becoming the regular catcher. He owns a .257/.335/.422 line over 688 games, a 104 OPS+.

The deal also gives top prospect Alfredo Duno more time to develop (you can read a full season review and scouting report on him right here). Signed out of Venezuela in January 2023, Duno, 20, ended 2026 in Double-A, yet with 181 games caught Gray says he needs more time.
