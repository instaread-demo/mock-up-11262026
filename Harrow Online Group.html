Christmas gift appeal launched for children at West Harrow primary school

November 25, 2026 8:19•2 min read

Residents and businesses are being asked to donate Christmas presents for children at Grange Primary School in West Harrow, where many families are facing financial hardship.

PCSO David Neal, from the West Harrow Safer Neighbourhoods Team, is helping organise the collection and is appealing for new, unwrapped gifts suitable for children aged four to 11. Toys, books, games and winter essentials are among the items being requested, with donations needed by 8 December.

Gifts can be dropped off at the school

Presents go to the front reception on Welbeck Road, West Harrow, HA2 0RY, giving organisers time to prepare them before Santa visits.

It follows a drive covered by Harrow Online last year; Neal welcomes gifts, cash or shares of the appeal.

Support for families facing hardship

Writing on Met Engage, Neal said many pupils' families are struggling and praised the staff's efforts.

A fundraising page accepts money, and businesses can offer sponsorship.
