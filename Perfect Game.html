The Future Looks Bright in SoCal

Scott Rankin

SoCal has always been a hot bed of talented baseball players throughout the years, but the class of 2029 might be as deep of a class as we have seen in some time.  I’ve highlighted a few standouts from the San Diego Section to follow.

Let’s start with three-headed monster over at Granite Hills High School led by Colton Windham. He is currently ranked the 15th overall player in the nation and 3rd ranked third baseman. At 6-foot-2, 190 pounds, the left-handed hitter batted .495 with 16 extra-base hits, including four homers, over 108 plate appearances as a freshman.

Jesse Maddox (6-foot, 190), No. 136 among outfielders nationally and No. 24 in the state, hit .267 with six doubles in 102 plate appearances in 2026 and has reached 86 on the mound.

Eagles teammate Jaden Macias (5-foot-8, 160) ranks 20th among the state’s shortstops, has caught and played second, has thrown up to 89 and hit .329 with four homers in 101 plate appearances.

Christian right-hander Talon Brown (6-foot-4, 205), the state’s 3rd-ranked RHP, has touched 91 with a 76-79 slider and went 4-1 with a 0.69 ERA and 45 strikeouts in 11 appearances (40.1 IP).

Barrett Grieve of The Bishop’s, a 6-foot-4, 185-pound infielder, hit .266 with two homers in 88 plate appearances and made the 15u WWBA West All-Tournament Team.

Other names to know include Wyatt Lo (Patrick Henry), Wyatt Gordon (Rancho Bernardo), Ryan Nicolaus (Eastlake), Hudson “Kane” Dejesus (Eastlake), Blake Ramos (San Marcos) and Parker Hyde (Coronado).
