Bodies of six aboard plane that crashed near Horn Island found in waters off Mississippi

BY CHAD CALDER | Staff writer Nov 24, 2026 Updated 1 hr ago 1 min to read

The bodies of six people whose plane crashed into the water off the Mississippi coast after leaving Lakefront Airport Tuesday have been found, the U.S. Coast Guard said.

The Cessna 210 aircraft lost contact with a regional air-traffic control tower in Mobile at about 3:45 p.m., prompting a search for the six people — four adults and two children — by local, state and federal agencies off of Horn Island.

Divers with the Mississippi Department of Marine Resources found all six inside the fuselage after a four-hour search, according to the Coast Guard.

Cmdr. Trevor Blount of Coast Guard Sector Mobile called it “a heartbreaking outcome,” and MDMR Executive Director Joe Spraggins extended condolences to the families.

The FAA and the NTSB will lead the investigation.

Bound for Fort Myers, Florida, the plane flew from Triumph Air Strip in Buras at 1:06 p.m. and reached Lakefront Airport at 1:29 p.m., FlightAware data shows. It is registered to an owner in Cape Coral, Florida.
