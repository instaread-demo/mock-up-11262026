COMPLETE NXT COVERAGE: NEW NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION, TLC IS SHAPING UP

Greetings, and welcome to PWInsider’s coverage of NXT live from Orlando on The CW in the U.S. and on Netflix here in Canada and internationally. Vic Joseph and Byron Saxton are on the call. 

As the show begins, we’re shown a split screen of NXT Women’s North American Champion Zaria and her challenger in tonight’s main event, Izzi Dame, each walking into the venue. 

The broadcast then revisits last week’s main event, where NXT Women’s Champion Kelani Jordan escaped Kendal Grey’s ankle lock by slapping the referee and taking a disqualification.

Grey, in street clothes, calls the escape cheap and vows to earn another title shot. Jordan replies that a suspension will delay any rematch.

General Manager Robert Stone notes a suspension would keep Jordan out of all WWE events, the John Cena Classic included, so he skips it and books a Grey vs. Jordan rematch under Tables, Ladders and Chairs rules for the TLC premium live event on Saturday, Dec. 12.

Backstage, NXT Champion Grayson Waller questions whether Mason Rook is ready for Shiloh Hill, which only motivates Rook ahead of their #1 contender’s bout.

Match #1: Eight-Person Tag Match - The Vanity Project (Brad Baylor, Jackson Drake, Ricky Smokes, Myka Lockwood) vs Hank & Tank, Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno

Myka Lockwood overpowers Noam Dar early, and her opponents keep tagging away from her as the bout turns into a brawl. Joseph mentions Booker T is on vacation, hence the recent fill-ins by Wade Barrett and Byron Saxton.

After the break, a punch from Tank misses Ricky Smokes and knocks his own partner Romeo Moreno off the apron. Lockwood takes over from there with a double clothesline, a superplex on Hank and a clothesline on Dar before her finisher earns the pin.

Your winners, The Vanity Project! 

An “Act 1” video package shows Izzi Dame mocking Zaria’s musical theatre background. In the locker room, Dame tells Emily Agard she will become a two-time North American Champion tonight, and Kali Armstrong and Lizzy Rain answer that they want title shots too.

Back in the ring, Jackson Drake hails Lockwood’s debut and knocks John Cena for picking Zilla Fatu over him for the Classic. Fatu challenges Drake for the North American Championship, Kam Hendrix, EK Prosper and Tristan Angels all stake claims too, and a brawl leaves Fatu and Prosper standing.

Waller plays the same mind games with Hill backstage, firing up both possible challengers. Elsewhere, Wren Sinclair’s plan to chase the Women’s North American title draws pushback from Rain and Armstrong.

Match #2: NXT Men’s Championship #1 Contender’s Match: Mason Rook vs Shiloh Hill

Rook controls much of the contest with power moves, sentons and a spinebuster, while Hill fights back with a bodyslam of his own and several near falls. Rook sends Hill ribs-first into the announce table and finishes him with a gut-wrench powerbomb and a moonsault.

Your winner, and the new #1 Contender for the Men’s NXT Championship, Mason Rook! 

The two shake hands afterward, but Waller bumps into Hill on the ramp. Hill rolls the champion into the ring, where Rook drops him with a Blue Thunder Bomb and raises the title.

Backstage, Chantel Monroe mocks Thea Hail in the makeup chair by exposing a bald spot, and Hail agrees and walks away. In a recorded promo, Myles Borne says a referee’s call cost him his last match with Tony D’Angelo and vows to replace him atop NXT, then finds a note from D’Angelo setting up a meeting next week.

In the arena, Hail talks about the pressure on NXT’s women to look a certain way and shares that, at 23, she has been diagnosed with alopecia, an autoimmune condition that causes hair loss. She says she has lost around 40 per cent of her hair in under four months and asks people to respect one another’s differences. She then shaves her head in the ring as the crowd rises for a standing ovation.

A second video package looks back at the history between Zaria and Dame.

Match #3: AAA World Trios Champions Perros Del Mal (Angel & Berto) with Daga and Karmen Petrovic vs BirthRight’s Uriah Connors and Channing “Stacks” Lorenzo

BirthRight keep Angel cut off early until Berto gets the tag, and Perros Del Mal turn things around, busting open Connors’ nose along the way. Berto’s moonsault attempt is interrupted when DarkState arrive through the crowd, and while the referee is distracted Cutler James hits Berto with a chair so Connors can score the pin with a knee.

Your winners, BirthRight!

Backstage, Sean Legacy challenges Saquon Shugars to a match next week, and John Cena offers John Cena Classic briefcases to Kendal Grey and Wren Sinclair.

Stone tells Emily Agard that next week brings a Fatal Four-Way between Fatu, Angels, Prosper and Hendrix for a shot at North American Champion Jackson Drake at TLC on Dec. 12, plus Waller vs. Rook and a Triple Threat with Dion Lennox, Daga and Lexis King.

Match #4: NXT Women’s North American Championship Match - Zaria (Champion) vs Izzi Dame (Challenger)

Zaria dominates the opening stretch, throwing Dame around inside and outside the ring and landing a suplex on the floor. The pair trade near falls, with Dame countering a super F5 into a codebreaker and later hitting a spear and a powerbomb, but Zaria survives each time.

Dame’s attempt to bring the title belt into play backfires when the referee takes it and Zaria spears her, though Dame reaches the ropes after an F5. Zaria then crashes into the ring steps, and Dame hits Dame Over twice before rolling her up for the three-count.

Your winner, and the new NXT Women’s North American Champion, Izzi Dame! 

Dame celebrates at ringside as Zaria looks on to close the show, with the TLC card still taking shape.
