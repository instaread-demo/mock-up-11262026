Dulwich Hamlet rock Isthmian League Premier Division leaders before late drama

DULWICH Hamlet were denied a win over the Isthmian League Premier Division leaders when Lekan Majoyegbe scored a dramatic 96th-minute equaliser for Welling United at Champion Hill.

Mark Dacy’s Hamlet arrested a run of three consecutive defeats, but their current sequence of results is one win in their last ten matches.

One point was enough to keep Welling on the top of the table and the draw extended their unbeaten league streak to six matches.

Lonit Talla put Hamlet ahead early from the spot after Nathan Cooper fouled Nyren Clunis, giving Mackenzie Foley no chance.

Welling, who had seen Zak O’Keefe strike the bar, drew level ten minutes before the break when Sam Smith sent a free-kick over the wall and past Hudson Sands.

Luke Wanadio restored the lead in the second half, tucking in the rebound after Foley saved from Sol Baugh.

Sands made several saves to keep the leaders out, but Majoyegbe headed home a Ryan Blackman corner deep in added time.

Dulwich Hamlet: Sands; Bonnett-Johnson, McKay, M Chambers, T Chambers (Bangura 80); Yeboah, Talla, (Wynter 71); Wanadio (Wade 77), Clunis, Baugh; Mills.

Substitutes not used: Smith, Obi.

Welling United: Foley; Otseh-Taiwo (Blackman 86), Cooper, Majoyegbe, Cooper, Newby; O’Keefe, Smith (Reynolds 51 (Miller-Rodney 90+3)); Anyadike, Bennett (Hambury 55), Rivera (Shomotun 70); Harris.

Substitutes not used: None.
