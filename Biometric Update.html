Italy opens digital ID wallet to public with legally valid driving licenses and IDs

IT-Wallet expands digital credentials as lawmakers propose social media age restrictions
Nov 24, 2026, 11:45 am EST | Chris Burt

Italy began opening its public IT-Wallet to all citizens and residents on November 23, expanding access to legally recognized digital documents through the IO public services app. The IT-Wallet ecosystem has graduated from beta testing by a random sample of people who use its IO digital public services app, beating the deadline for general availability by a month.

IO users with a biometric electronic identity card (CIE) can opt in early, storing an mDL, the European Disability Charter, a European Health Insurance Card and more in the IT-Wallet.

The mDL is valid ID and driving proof at police checks, verified via the Valida Documenti app; all documents legally equal their paper versions.

Private firms began testing in May, ahead of the framework’s July approval.

Undersecretary Alessio Butti called IO an interim step and IT-Wallet “a leap forward.”

Denmark’s AltID and France Titres are the only other live EUDI Wallets, with the December 24 deadline near.

Documents in IO stay until July 2027; Butti says 13 million Italians have activated them. PagoPA S.p.A. runs IO and the public wallet; private EUDI Wallets should follow.

The data protection regulator cleared its technical details in October.

Social media restrictions proposed

A proposed Security Decree amendment would require wallet-compatible age checks on social media and video-sharing platforms, barring under-13s and making 13- to 15-year-olds link accounts to a parent.

Center-right lawmakers filed it, La Capitale reports. AGCOM would enforce it; no method is set, and accounts eight-plus years old are exempt.

The EDPS recently urged a ban on facial age estimation in the EU KIDS Act.
