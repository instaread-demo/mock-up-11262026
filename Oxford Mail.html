Village pub reopens under Marco Pierre White's former chef

25th November Marco Pierre White Food and drink Leisure Pubs and bars
By James Richings News and Sports Reporter

A cosy village pub across the border in Buckinghamshire has now reopened under one of Marco Pierre White's former chefs from Oxfordshire.

The Stag in Flackwell Heath welcomed locals, families and visitors on Sunday, November 22, as new owners Dan Roth, 30, and Sophie Barnett, 25, officially launched their first day in business.

Since getting the keys earlier this month, the pair have spent weeks on improvements, helped by friends, family and ex-colleagues.

The pub has changed hands four times in five years, yet it remains a village fixture, and plenty of residents came to back its return. Nerves aside, day one went well.

Sophie Barnett and Dan Roth are now in charge of the Stag (Image: NQ)

Mr Roth, who is from Banbury, said a leaking coffee machine caused a brief scare shortly before opening, but it was soon sorted. Ms Barnett said the family crowd was "exactly what we wanted".

The Stag is based in Flackwell Heath has now opened (Image: NQ)

A birthday party booked by people following the pub's progress on social media added to what staff called one of its busiest Sundays in years, and Mr Roth praised his brand-new team.

The couple have revealed they have had their first booking for Christmas (Image: NQ)

The menu centres on homemade British pub classics and Sunday roasts using seasonal ingredients.

Next, the couple aim to build Sunday trade and get ready for Christmas, with their first Christmas Day booking already taken, as they work to restore confidence in the pub.
